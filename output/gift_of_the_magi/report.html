📖 Plot Summary

The story opens on Christmas Eve in a modest, shabby apartment where Della Young, a devoted wife, counts her meager savings of one dollar and eighty-seven cents. Overcome with sorrow at her inability to afford a worthy gift for her husband, Jim, she makes a desperate decision driven by love. She sells her most prized possession, her long, beautiful brown hair, to Madame Sofronie for twenty dollars, which she immediately uses to purchase a platinum fob chain intended for Jim's heirloom gold watch. Upon Jim's return home, the atmosphere shifts as he stares in stunned silence at Della's newly shorn hair. In a moment of reciprocal sacrifice, Jim reveals that he has sold his own gold watch to purchase a set of expensive tortoise shell combs for Della's hair, rendering both their gifts currently unusable. The couple realizes the futility of their actions, as the chain has no watch to attach to and the combs have no hair to adorn, yet their mutual devotion remains the central focus of the scene. The narrative concludes with the couple embracing, transcending the material loss through their shared act of giving. The narrator reflects on their situation, describing Della and Jim as the wisest of gift-givers. Despite the apparent irony of their exchanged sacrifices, the story affirms that their love and willingness to give up their most cherished possessions for one another represent the true essence of the holiday spirit.

Themes: sacrifice, love, materialism, wisdom
