📖 Plot Summary

During the carnival season at dusk, Montresor encounters his acquaintance Fortunato, who is dressed in a motley jester costume and heavily intoxicated. Seizing the opportunity, Montresor lures Fortunato into the family catacombs beneath the Montresor palazzo by claiming to have doubts about a pipe of Amontillado wine. As they descend on foot through winding staircases into the damp, nitre-encrusted vaults, Montresor feigns concern for Fortunato's worsening cough while repeatedly offering him wine to ensure his compliance. This manipulation allows Montresor to guide the unsuspecting Fortunato deeper into the crypts under the guise of wine tasting. Upon reaching a deep crypt, the situation turns violent as Montresor chains the unsuspecting Fortunato to a granite wall inside a small niche. Ignoring the victim's transition from drunken laughter to frantic screams and desperate pleas, Montresor begins walling him in with stone and mortar. The narrative details the grim process of entombment, where Montresor continues his work despite the victim's escalating distress, ensuring that Fortunato cannot escape the fate Montresor has prepared for him within the underground vaults. The chapter concludes with the narrator finishing the masonry, covering the niche with a rampart of bones to seal the crime. Montresor notes that no one has disturbed the remains for half a century, indicating the success and permanence of his revenge. This retrospective account confirms that the entombment was never discovered, leaving Fortunato's fate sealed within the dark catacombs long after the events of the carnival night occurred.

Themes: revenge, deception, secrecy
