📖 Plot Summary

During the carnival season at dusk, Montresor encounters his friend Fortunato, who is dressed in a motley jester costume and heavily intoxicated. Seizing the opportunity, Montresor lures Fortunato into the Montresor family catacombs by claiming to have doubts about a pipe of Amontillado wine. This initial interaction sets the stage for a descent into the underground vaults, where Montresor feigns concern for Fortunato's worsening cough while guiding him deeper into the damp and dangerous environment. As they navigate the winding staircases and damp vaults filled with nitre and human remains, Montresor employs various tactics to keep Fortunato compliant. He offers the friend Medoc and De Grave wine to maintain his cooperation, despite the deteriorating conditions. This manipulation continues until they reach a deep crypt, where Montresor's true intentions become clear as he chains Fortunato to a granite wall within a small niche. The story reaches its grim conclusion as Montresor begins to wall Fortunato in with stone and mortar, ignoring the friend's initial confusion and subsequent desperate pleas for mercy. Montresor completes the masonry that seals Fortunato inside, hearing only the jingling of Fortunato's bells in the dark before re-erecting a rampart of bones against the new wall. This final act leaves the fate of Fortunato sealed within the crypt.

Themes: manipulation, betrayal, death
