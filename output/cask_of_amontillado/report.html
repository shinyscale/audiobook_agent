📖 Plot Summary

During the carnival season at dusk, Montresor encounters his friend Fortunato, who is dressed in a motley jester costume and heavily intoxicated. Seizing the opportunity, Montresor lures Fortunato away from the festivities by feigning concern over a pipe of Amontillado wine he believes to be a fraud. This deception sets the stage for their journey as Montresor guides the unsuspecting Fortunato from the safety of his palazzo down a long, winding staircase into the damp, nitre-encrusted catacombs of the Montresors. As the two men descend deeper into the crypts, Montresor repeatedly feigns concern for Fortunato's worsening cough and offers him wine to steady his nerves, further masking his true intentions. Upon reaching a deep crypt, the situation turns fatal as Montresor chains the unsuspecting Fortunato to a granite wall within a small recess. Montresor begins to wall him in with stone and mortar, ignoring the victim's initial confusion, subsequent screams, and final pleas for mercy as the trap is sprung. As Montresor completes the final tier of the wall, only the jingling of Fortunato's bells remains audible before Montresor expresses a sense of sickness. The story concludes with Montresor sealing the tomb and re-erecting the old rampart of bones, having successfully executed his revenge without detection during the chaotic carnival season.

Themes: revenge, deception, cruelty
