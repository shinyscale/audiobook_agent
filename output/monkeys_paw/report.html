📖 Plot Summary

On a cold, wet night at the isolated Laburnam Villa, Mr. White, his wife Mrs. White, and their son Herbert share a tense, quiet evening marked by emotional distance and unspoken frustrations. Their solitude is shattered by the arrival of Sergeant-Major Morris, a weary traveler who brings with him a dried monkey’s paw rumored to grant three wishes—each cursed by devastating consequences. Though Morris urgently warns them to destroy it, Mr. White, intrigued and defiant, retrieves the paw from the fire. In a moment of dark humor, Herbert jokes about wishing for money, prompting Mr. White to reluctantly wish for two hundred pounds; the paw twists violently in his hand, leaving the family shaken but dismissive as the wind howls outside. The next morning, the Whites try to laugh off the night’s events, treating the paw as a superstition, until the grim reality of their wish unfolds. A somber representative from Maw and Meggins arrives to inform them that Herbert has died in a machinery accident—and the company offers exactly two hundred pounds in compensation. The chilling coincidence shatters Mr. White’s skepticism and collapses him into unconsciousness, revealing the paw’s terrifying power. Grief overtakes the household, isolating the couple in their sorrow as they retreat from the world, haunted by the echo of their son’s laughter and the weight of their careless wish. Driven by unbearable grief, Mrs. White insists her husband use the paw to bring Herbert back to life. Terrified but powerless against her anguish, Mr. White makes the second wish—and the house is plunged into an unnatural silence, broken only by a slow, deliberate knock at the front door. As Mrs. White rushes to open it, convinced it is her son, Mr. White realizes the horror of what they’ve summoned: a mangled, unnatural version of Herbert. In a final act of desperate love, he scrambles for the paw and makes his third wish just as the bolt is drawn back. When he stumbles outside, the street is empty—no trace of the knock, no sign of his son, only the chilling stillness of a world that has been forever altered by their meddling with forces beyond comprehension.

Themes: loss, grief, the danger of tampering with fate
